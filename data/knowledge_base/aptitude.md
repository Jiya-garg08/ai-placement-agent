# Aptitude and Quantitative Reasoning

## Permutations and Combinations
- Factorial: n! = n * (n-1) * ... * 1. (0! = 1).
- Permutation (Order matters): P(n, r) = n! / (n - r)!. Used when arrangement, sequences, or position designations matter.
- Combination (Order does not matter): C(n, r) = n! / (r! * (n - r)!). Used for selecting teams, groups, or subsets.

## Probability Foundations
- Basic Probability: P(E) = (Number of Favorable Outcomes) / (Total Number of Exhaustive Outcomes).
- Complementary Event: P(E') = 1 - P(E).
- Independent Events: P(A ∩ B) = P(A) * P(B).
- Conditional Probability: P(A | B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B).
- Bayes' Theorem: P(A | B) = [P(B | A) * P(A)] / P(B).

## Work, Time, and Rates
- Work Equivalence: If a person completes a task in D days, their 1-day rate of work is 1/D.
- Combined Work Rate: If Person A works at rate 1/A and Person B works at rate 1/B, together their combined daily rate is (1/A + 1/B), completing the entire task in 1 / (1/A + 1/B) = (A * B) / (A + B) days.
- Pipe and Cistern: Inflow pipes contribute positive work (+1/T); outflow/leak pipes contribute negative work (-1/T).
